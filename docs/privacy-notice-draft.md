## What this notice covers

This notice explains how we use personal data when someone verifies a FUR product, interacts with our services, contacts us, or acts as an authorised administrator or inspector. It does not yet describe processing that has not been designed or approved.

## Information we may use

Depending on the service used, we may process:

- verification identifiers, including an opaque scan/request ID and the identifier assigned to a FUR tag;
- verification result and time;
- administrator or inspector account identifiers, roles and authorised actions;
- product and inspection identifiers, inspection results, evidence hashes or controlled evidence references;
- communications and information supplied when contacting us;
- essential security and service logs that our hosting, database or authentication providers generate, which may include IP address, device/browser information and event times.

FUR should not place names or other personal information in product descriptions, audit-detail fields or evidence-reference text unless this is necessary, documented and approved.
