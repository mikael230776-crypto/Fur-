## 1. Parties and order of precedence

These terms govern access to FUR services by the business identified in an accepted order form (Business). The order form, any data-processing terms, service description and these terms form the agreement. The following precedence is proposed: signed order form; data-processing addendum for personal-data issues; service-specific terms; these terms. [CONFIRM.]

## 2. Service

FUR may provide product registration, NFC-based verification, security monitoring, administration tools, physical-inspection records and related support as specified in the order form. Features described as beta, disabled, draft or pending supplier acceptance are not part of the service unless expressly activated in writing.

A verification result reflects the records, cryptographic checks and evidence available at the time. It is not an absolute guarantee of provenance, safety, legal title, condition, value, regulatory compliance or future authenticity. The Business must not represent otherwise.

## 3. Accounts, permissions and security

The Business must:

- appoint authorised users and keep their permissions at the minimum necessary level;
- provide accurate account information and promptly remove access when a user changes role or leaves;
- protect credentials, devices, recovery methods, tags and secrets;
- not share administrator accounts or attempt to bypass access, rate, security or physical-authentication controls;
- notify FUR promptly of suspected compromise, loss, misuse or unauthorised verification activity;
- ensure its users and contractors comply with the agreement.

FUR may require multi-factor or physical authentication for sensitive functions. Administrator and inspector actions may be logged for security and accountability.

## 4. Product and tag responsibilities

The Business is responsible for lawful product information and for ensuring it has authority to register or manage each product. It must not:

- submit false, misleading, infringing, unlawful or unsafe product information;
- place personal data in product descriptions, tag identifiers, audit-detail fields or evidence references unless expressly approved and necessary;
- clone, alter, remove, reuse, transfer or tamper with a tag contrary to the approved operating procedure;
- claim that FUR certification replaces legal, safety, quality or industry-specific obligations;
- use verification results to make unlawful or unfair decisions about individuals.

The Business must follow issued handling, inspection, replacement, suspension and incident procedures.

## 5. Acceptable use

The Business must not misuse the service, probe it without written permission, introduce malicious code, overload it, scrape it, reverse engineer protected elements except where law cannot exclude that right, access another customer’s data, or use it for unlawful surveillance, discrimination, fraud or infringement.

## 6. Suspension

FUR may suspend affected access or products where reasonably necessary to address a security incident, suspected fraud, legal or regulatory requirement, non-payment, material breach, risk to users or infrastructure, or misuse of a tag or account. Where lawful and practicable, FUR will give notice and limit suspension to what is necessary. Urgent protective action may occur without advance notice. Suspension does not determine ownership or authenticity and must be reviewable through a documented process.

## 7. Data protection

Each party must comply with applicable data-protection law. The parties must determine and document whether each acts as an independent controller, joint controller or processor for each processing activity before service launch.

Where FUR acts as processor, an Article 28-compliant data-processing addendum must be signed before processing. It must address documented instructions, confidentiality, security, subprocessors, international transfers, rights assistance, breach/DPIA assistance, deletion or return, and audits.

The Business must give required privacy information, establish a lawful basis, minimise submitted data, handle individual requests within its responsibility and not instruct FUR to process unlawfully. Approved retention periods and the current FUR privacy notice will apply. [COMPLETE ROLES, PROCESSORS, LOCATIONS AND TRANSFER TERMS.]

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party must protect the other’s confidential information using reasonable care and use it only for the agreement. Permitted disclosures should be limited to people who need it and are bound by confidentiality, or disclosures required by law after notice where lawful. Standard exclusions for public, previously known, independently developed and lawfully received information require professional review.

## 9. Intellectual property

Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. Subject to payment and compliance, FUR grants the Business a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the service during the term for internal business purposes. Ownership and permitted use of product content, feedback, aggregated statistics, marks, APIs and generated outputs must be completed in the order form or reviewed clauses. No right to use FUR branding or issue certification claims is granted unless expressly written.

## 10. Charges and tax

Charges, currency, invoicing, payment period, renewal, usage limits, expenses, VAT/tax treatment, disputed invoices and late-payment rights must be stated in the order form. No automatic renewal or price-change mechanism should be used until professionally reviewed and clearly disclosed. [COMMERCIAL TERMS REQUIRED.]

## 11. Availability, support and changes

Service levels, support hours, maintenance, backups, recovery objectives and remedies apply only if written in an order form or service-level agreement. FUR may make proportionate security or technical changes, but material reductions require notice and any agreed remedy. [SLA REQUIRED OR EXPRESSLY EXCLUDED.]

## 15. Term and termination

The order form must state the initial term and renewal position. Either party may terminate for an unremedied material breach after an appropriate cure period, insolvency where lawful, or any expressly agreed convenience right. On termination, access ends; unpaid sums become due; data is returned or deleted under the approved schedule and data-processing terms; and clauses intended to survive remain effective.
